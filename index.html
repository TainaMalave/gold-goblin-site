How it works

Set it once, then forget about it

Pick a price, and Gold Goblin keeps an eye on the token for you.

  1. Add Gold Goblin

    Invite it to your server in two clicks. It only asks to view channels, send messages and embed links.

  2. Set a target

    Run /myalert add for a DM, or /tokenalert add to post in a channel. Choose "drops to or below" or "rises to or above" a price.

  3. Get alerted

    Gold Goblin checks the price every 5 minutes and alerts you once each time it crosses your target. No spam while it sits there.

Features

Everything you need to time your token

Built for guilds and solo players alike.

Live token price

/token shows the current price for the US, EU, KR or TW region. You can also just DM the bot "price".

Alerts in your DMs

Set up to 10 personal alerts with /myalert. Gold Goblin DMs you the moment the price crosses your target.

Server alerts with role pings

Admins can post alerts to any channel and ping a role like @Token Watchers. Each server can have up to 25 alerts.

Your region, remembered

Set a default region for the whole server with /settings, or just for yourself with /mysettings.

Once, or every time

Alerts fire each time the price crosses your target. Set once and the alert removes itself after it's delivered.

No clutter

Pick alerts to remove from a list. When a channel, role or server goes away, its alerts are cleaned up automatically.
