Privacy Policy

Effective October 27, 2026

In short: Gold Goblin only stores the Discord IDs and settings it needs to deliver your alerts. It doesn't store your messages, sell your data, or track you. You can delete your data yourself at any time.

This policy explains what the Gold Goblin Discord bot ("Gold Goblin", "the bot", "we") collects when you use it, why, and how to remove it.

What we store

  • Discord IDs: your Discord user ID when you set a personal alert or a default region. For server alerts, the server, channel and role IDs, plus the user ID of the person who set the alert.
  • Alert settings: the direction ("drops to or below" or "rises to or above"), the target price, the region, whether the alert is one-time, and whether it has already fired.
  • Default regions: the region a server picks with /settings region, or a person picks with /mysettings region.

What we don't store

  • Message content. When you DM the bot, it reads the message only to reply, and never saves it.
  • Names, emails, passwords or payment details. The bot never asks for them.
  • Price history. WoW Token prices from Blizzard are only kept in memory for about a minute.
  • Tracking or analytics. Neither the bot nor this website uses cookies, analytics or ads.

How we use it

Only to deliver your alerts and remember your settings. We don't sell, rent or share your data with anyone.

Third parties

  • Discord: Gold Goblin runs on Discord, and your use of Discord is covered by Discord's Privacy Policy.
  • Blizzard: the bot requests public WoW Token prices from Blizzard's API. No information about you is sent to Blizzard.
  • Hosting: the bot and its data run on a privately operated server. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may log technical information such as IP addresses, as described in GitHub's Privacy Statement.

How long we keep it

Data is kept until it's removed, either by you or automatically:

  • Remove a personal alert with /myalert remove, and clear your region with /mysettings region → "Clear my setting".
  • Server admins can remove server alerts with /tokenalert remove, and clear the server's region with /settings region.
  • When Gold Goblin is removed from a server, that server's alerts and settings are deleted automatically. Deleting a channel deletes its alerts.
  • One-time alerts are deleted after they're delivered.

Children

Gold Goblin is meant for people who meet Discord's minimum age requirement in their country.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.

Contact

For questions or data requests, open an issue on GitHub. Issues are public, so don't include personal details there.
